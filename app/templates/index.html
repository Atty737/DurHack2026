Welcome to the Weekly Meal Planner

This page explains the functionality of the website.

Recipes

Recipes allows you to generate a recipe for every dinner of the week. It generates unique meals using items in your pantry and keeps within your budget for any extra items.

Scan Receipt

Scan Receipt allows you to take a photo of a receipt from any supermarket and it then extracts the food items you bought and adds them to your pantry. This is then used to help generate recipes

Pantry

This displays all the ingredients you currently have in your pantry. You can delete all ingredients to clear your pantry or delete ingredients as you use them

My Recipes

This displays all the recipes you have currently generated. When you have made a recipe you can tick it off in this menu

Nearest Shop

This displays the shops in order of distance to your location. It shows these on whilst showing where you are
